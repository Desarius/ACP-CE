# ACP-CE Research Roadmap

**Conceptual proposer:** Dario Visaggio "Desarius"  
**Status:** Proposed phases, not completed experiments

## Phase 0: Definitions and prior art

Formalize continuous experience, plasticity, persistent change and individualization. Complete a systematic literature review, preregister hypotheses and specify safety constraints.

**Gate:** Reproducible, criticizable protocol and agreed metrics.

## Phase 1: Baseline comparison

Compare frozen stateless agents with otherwise matched frozen agents using episodic retrieval. Record prompt, context, model and compute.

**Gate:** Reproducible distinction between retrieval and session effects.

## Phase 2: Plasticity mechanisms

Add carefully tracked update mechanisms such as adapters, trained policies or learned skills, with memory-access ablations and held-out transfer tasks.

**Gate:** Durable effects beyond straightforward autobiographical recall.

## Phase 3: Self-directed cognition

Compare bounded agent-initiated reflection/exploration with compute-matched scheduled reflection and inactive controls. Include calibration and failure measurements.

**Gate:** Confirmed benefit or a documented negative result.

## Phase 4: Developmental histories

Expose initially identical agents to different experiences. Evaluate blinded, cross-domain tests of stable but revisable behavioral differences.

**Gate:** History-related effects that survive confound checks.

## Phase 5: Longitudinal validation

Extend to longer real-world intervals only after earlier gates. Treat model upgrades and environment changes as interventions, not invisible maintenance.

**Gate:** Independently replicable longitudinal findings.

At every phase use bounded permissions, logging, privacy safeguards, and an available means of safely pausing or stopping the experiment.
