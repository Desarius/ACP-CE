## Further review priorities

- Lifelong learning and stability-plasticity tradeoffs
- Developmental robotics and intrinsic motivation
- Autobiographical memory, episodic reflection and persistent agents
- Separating external retrieval from changes in learned parameters or policy
- Longitudinal and open-ended learning evaluations
- Ethics of experimental autonomy and oversight

**Integrity note:** These sources predate ACP-CE and must be credited independently; they do not validate the ACP-CE hypotheses.
