# ACP-CE

**Artificial Cognitive Plasticity through Continuous Experience**  
*A Research Manifesto for Lifelong Cognitive Development in Artificial Agents*

**Author and conceptual proposer:** Dario Visaggio "Desarius"  
**Status:** Research proposal · v0.1 · November 3, 2026 · Not peer reviewed

> The central question is not only what an artificial agent remembers, but how its experiences change what it becomes.

ACP-CE proposes the controlled study of artificial agents whose cognitive dispositions, strategies and capabilities are durably altered by continuous experience. It does **not** claim to demonstrate consciousness, personhood or the emergence of human-like identity.

## Core research questions

1. Can agents develop transferable adaptations that persist even without retrieval of their original experiences?
2. Does the ordering and continuity of experiences affect subsequent learning and problem-solving?
3. Can bounded spontaneous cognitive activity improve learning beyond compute-matched scheduled work?
4. Can developmental histories generate stable yet revisable differences between initially identical agents?

The research questions require controlled baselines, ablations, pre-registered metrics and independent replication. Prior work in continual learning, developmental robotics, cognitive architectures and autonomous agents must be credited.
